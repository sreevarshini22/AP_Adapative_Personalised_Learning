Variational Circuit Architecture

1. AngleEmbedding: Maps normalized student continuous values into quantum state amplitudes on 5 Hilbert space wires.

2. Parameterized Ansatz: 2 layers of 3-axis Euler Rotations (`Rot(phi, theta, omega)`) parameterized by trained weight tensors.

3. CNOT Ring Entanglement: Inter-qubit circular entanglement capturing correlations between attendance, math, and programming abilities.
